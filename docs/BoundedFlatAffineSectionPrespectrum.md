# The actual affine section prespectrum

COL-0355 applies COL-0352 and COL-0353 to the actual native bounded-above-flat geometric
pseudofunctor. No supplied category-valued profile or old Model adapter is used.

For every commutative ring R, the category is the full category of strongly cocartesian
strict sections over Under R of the original Grothendieck projection. Evaluation is the
original identity evaluation followed by the actual original-fiber functor. Its proved
categorical equivalence retains the original geometric pullback section at every coefficient
arrow. Evaluation of that canonical section uses the original pseudofunctor identity
isomorphism, not an asserted literal identity of arbitrary complexes.

The chosen-zero section restricts one global family of existing fiber zero objects, independent
of the source ring. Both evaluation and coefficient restriction preserve these choices by
literal equality. Genuine transition isomorphisms and arbitrary exact bounded-flat geometric
pullback identify the pointwise cofibration/weak-equivalence classes with identity-evaluated
classes. The actual evaluation equivalence transports Waldhausen pushouts and axioms. Every
evaluation and every coefficient restriction preserves pushouts along cofibrations; no
preservation of all geometric pushouts is assumed.

Sections have object and hom universe u + 1. They are bundled directly as native small
Waldhausen categories, with their original chosen zero. Literal restriction identity and
composition and chosen-zero equality prove the strict coefficient model functor before
composition with the native iterated-S prespectrum functor. The induced zero-space map is
the unchanged coefficient K-map, including degree zero. The consumer proves ZMod 2 is not
flat over the integers and still constructs its actual exact coefficient restriction, then
composes with the nonidentity diagonal ZMod 2 → ZMod 2 × ZMod 2.

negative Bass localization and Thomason–Trobaugh 7.4 are not consequences claimed by this item.
