# Scalar input descent and the relative boundary

COL-0329 completes the connective relative-open scalar boundary topic.
Read COL-0320 and COL-0324 first for the original cube return and the actual
normalized and raw operations. COL-0329 then supplies:

1. Forward horizontal subdivision followed by the diagonal realization covers
   every original K-class, including degree zero.
2. The literal scalar projection input is surjective. Its universal-property and
   homomorphism-extensionality forms allow descent without choosing an inverse.
3. Both original scalar operations have the positive supported scalar-cone
   boundary as full homomorphisms in every natural degree, including zero.

The assumptions are an open immersion, a scalar, a specified unit on the open
and their restriction equality. No extra coverage, boundary, flatness or qcqs
hypothesis is imposed. The original maps and positive orientation are retained.
The boundary uses PerfectOpenK for the relative coarse Waldhausen structure;
this is not an identification with fine perfect K-theory of the open scheme.
Fine/global qcqs transport and Bass or negative-degree boundaries remain open.
